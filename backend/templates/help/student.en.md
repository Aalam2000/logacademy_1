# How to work on the platform

## Home — my lessons

On the [Home](/dashboard) — all your lessons. At the top — the name of your group and the button to enter the video conference.

Lessons can be viewed in two ways:

- **Table** — a list of lessons that the teacher has opened. Today's lesson is marked "Today."
- **Calendar** — all group lessons by dates. Gray lessons have not yet been opened by the teacher.

A lesson where something is required from you is highlighted:

- **Homework: submit** (📝) — homework has not yet been submitted;
- **Homework: revise** (↺) — the teacher returned the answer for revision;
- **Messages** (💬) — new messages from the teacher.

Click on the lesson to open it.

If you have unsubmitted homework, a colored bar with the lesson name and deadline is visible under the header on any page: orange — homework is awaiting a response or returned for revision, red — the submission deadline has passed. The link **Open lesson** leads directly to the assignment. The bar disappears as soon as you attach your response.

## Lesson

When the lesson starts, a question **"How do you like the lesson?"** appears at the top right along with three smileys: green — liked it, yellow — okay, red — did not like it. Click on one of them. The rating can be changed. The teacher does not see who selected which smiley.

A lesson marked 👤 — is personal: only you and those invited by the teacher can see it.

Below are three tabs: **Materials**, **Homework**, and **Dialogue**. The number on the tab indicates that something is waiting for you there.

### Materials

Files and links for the lesson. Click on the title — the material will open in a new tab. Below the list — notes from the teacher about the lesson.

### Homework — homework assignment

1. Click on the assignment title — the file will download. The deadline is indicated next to it.
2. Complete the assignment and click **+ File** to upload your answer. You can upload multiple files.
3. As long as the teacher has not checked the answer and the deadline has not passed, the file can be deleted (✕) and a new one uploaded.

Answer status:

- **Not submitted** — you have not uploaded an answer yet;
- **Waiting for review** — the answer is with the teacher;
- **Returned for revision** — correct it and upload a new file, details in the "Dialogue";
- **Accepted** or **Grade** — the answer has been checked, it cannot be changed anymore;
- **Not submitted, deadline expired** — the deadline has passed.

### Dialogue

Correspondence with the teacher for this lesson. Write a message and send it. You can edit your message — the **Edit** button.

## Performance

In the [Performance](/dashboard/performance) section — all your grades for lessons, homework, and exams, as well as absences. You can see the marks for each lesson here — they are not on the lesson page itself. An absence can be closed: if you attended an additional (personal) lesson after it, it is marked as "Absence closed" and does not count against you.

## Profile

In the [Profile](/dashboard/profile) you can change your password.
